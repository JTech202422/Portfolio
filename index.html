Cookbook layoutEditorial design

NIGERIAN DELIGHT

Brief

Design an inviting, easy-to-follow cookbook that makes Nigerian delicacies feel approachable for home cooks.

Approach

I built a clear grid, paired a Rockwell headline font with a clean body font, and used Black and Green colour palette to keep recipes readable and food photography as the focus.

Result

A 7-page layout with consistent recipe pages, chapter openers and a cover that fits the brand.

Tools: [InDesign / Photoshop]

About me

I'm Jackson, a graphic designer based in Nigeria. I design editorial layouts, packaging, social media ads, flyers, invitations, email campaigns and many more, mainly in Photoshop, Illustrator and InDesign. Canva included.

Before design, I studied law and worked in hospitality, virtual assistance and travel management, including three years on the J-1 Work & Travel program in the US. That experience taught me to listen closely to what people need, work to deadlines and pay attention to detail, and I bring the same habits to every brief.

I'm looking for a graphic designer role where I can keep growing and deliver clear, well-finished work.
